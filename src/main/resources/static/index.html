Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pharmacist verification, medical data encryption, and 24/7 emergency support on MediLink.

Every clinical pharmacist on MediLink undergoes a structured verification protocol: pharmacists register their official DGDA license number, which is audited by clinical administrators alongside dispensary affiliations prior to consultation activation. Additionally, automated direct API sync with DGDA and Pharmacy Council of Bangladesh (PCB) national registries is planned on our active product roadmap for upcoming real-time registry lookups.

Yes. MediLink is built strictly following HIPAA data privacy standards and enterprise AES-256 bank-grade encryption both in transit and at rest. Your prescription uploads, dosage logs, and tele-consult notes are exclusively accessible by you and authorized healthcare providers you explicitly grant consent to.

Our client-side Tesseract neural OCR engine extracts medication names, potencies, and frequencies directly from uploaded doctor prescription slips. It then cross-references the therapeutic active ingredients against our curated pharmacology interaction matrix to flag hazardous drug-drug interactions and dosage anomalies before fulfillment.

MediLink is 100% free forever for patients, including daily dose scheduling, offline medication alarms, and record vault storage. Partner pharmacy dispensaries and hospital networks access advanced order queues, inventory telemetry, and verified tele-consult portals through transparent professional subscription tiers.

The Emergency 24/7 Finder utilizes GPS geolocation paired with live night-dispensing telemetry from partner pharmacies. It immediately identifies the nearest facility within a 15km radius that is actively open, confirms essential stock availability (e.g. insulin, oxygen, inhalers), and provides direct one-tap tele-calling.
